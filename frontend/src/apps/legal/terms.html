The service

BaboReborn is a free browser multiplayer arena shooter with no advertising, operated by Paco Ricci. Play on community servers as a guest or sign in with Google for account features.

For support and reports, contact baboreborn@gmail.com.

Play rules

  • Do not cheat, disrupt matches, attack servers or bypass access restrictions.
  • Do not harass other players, impersonate others or expose their private information.
  • Use appropriate player and server names, and respect the rights of others.
  • Follow the rules of the community server you choose.

Community servers and moderation

Community servers are independently operated. Their operators manage rooms, moderation and access. Check the selected server's rules and privacy information before joining.

Availability and accounts

The game is under active development. Maintenance, updates and connection failures can interrupt play. Community operators determine their own server availability.

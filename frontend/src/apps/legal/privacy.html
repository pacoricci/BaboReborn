Privacy & cookies

Updated 4 October 2026

Who we are

Paco Ricci runs the official BaboReborn portal. The game is free and has no advertising. Contact: baboreborn@gmail.com.

What data we use

Google sign-in gives us an account identifier. We store it with your portal account and sessions to let you sign in. We do not request your email or contacts.

Game servers receive your account or guest identifier, nickname, appearance, gameplay activity and connection data, including your IP address. Other players see your nickname, appearance and match activity. Server operators also keep ownership and moderation records.

Cookies and saved preferences

Cookies support sign-in: sessions last up to 30 days and temporary login checks up to 10 minutes. Your browser also saves preferences, favorite rooms and guest identifiers until you clear site data, plus temporary match recovery data for the tab's session.

Other services

A hosting provider supplies the infrastructure for the portal.

Google handles Google sign-in under its privacy policy. Community servers are run by independent operators, with their own privacy and moderation practices. Your browser connects directly to the server you choose.

Data retention

Portal accounts are deleted after 24 months without signing in. Technical logs are kept for up to 14 days and backups for up to 30 days from creation. Expired sessions and login checks are deleted automatically.

You can delete your portal account from Account, after resolving any server ownership or transfers. This removes your Google sign-in link and portal sessions; access to games already joined may continue for up to 15 minutes.

Deleting your account does not erase community server records, browser preferences or existing backups, which expire within 30 days from creation.

Your rights

Email baboreborn@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your data. Where applicable, you can also request restriction or portability, object to processing or withdraw consent. You can complain to a data protection authority, including the Italian Garante.
